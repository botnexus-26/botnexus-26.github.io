Support

Report an error.

Found something that isn't working correctly? Send the details to the BotNexus team and we'll take a look.

Error report

Keep it simple. Tell us what went wrong and where it happened.

Used to identify the report and ping you when it reaches our support channel.
Reports are reviewed by the BotNexus team.

Before submitting

Make sure the report clearly explains the problem. You don't need to write a technical explanation.

Severity guide

  • Low — small issue or cosmetic problem.
  • Medium — feature is partially affected.
  • High — important feature is not working.
  • Critical — major BotNexus functionality is unavailable.
